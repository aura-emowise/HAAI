# HealthAudit AI: Understand. Pay Less. Dispute Boldly. 💡
(https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)


> **Your personal Financial Auditor in Healthcare**  
> An AI-powered tool that decodes insurance policies, predicts medical costs, and audits bills — so you can make informed decisions and fight unfair charges.


---

## 🌟 Overview

Medical billing is broken. Patients receive cryptic, inflated bills while insurance policies read like legal contracts. **HealthAudit AI** empowers users to take control of their healthcare spending by leveraging large language models (LLMs) to:

1. **Decode** complex insurance documents
2. **Predict** out-of-pocket costs before treatment
3. **Audit** medical bills for errors and overcharging

Built for the modern patient — no finance degree required.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔍 Step 1: Policy Decoder
- Upload your insurance policy (PDF, DOC, text)
- Get a plain-language summary of coverage, deductibles, copays, and exclusions
- Ask natural language questions:  
  _"Does my plan cover an MRI?"_  
  _"How much will I pay for a $15,000 surgery?"_

### 💰 Step 2: Cost Predictor
- Enter a procedure (e.g., "Knee Arthroscopy"), ICD-10 code, and location
- Estimate total cost using public hospital price transparency data
- Break down charges and calculate **your personal out-of-pocket cost** based on your plan

### 📄 Step 3: Bill Auditor
- Upload a medical bill (scan or PDF)
- AI decodes CPT, HCPCS, and ICD codes into plain English
- Detects common billing errors:
  - Double billing
  - Unbundling
  - Overpriced services
- Generates a **ready-to-send dispute letter** to the hospital or insurer

## 🖱️ How to Use (Demo)

1. Clone or download this repo
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser
3. Navigate through the three steps:
   - Upload a sample insurance policy
   - Predict cost for a procedure
   - Audit a sample medical bill
4. Click buttons to see AI-powered insights and generated appeal letters

> 🔐 **Note**: This is a frontend prototype. No data is uploaded or stored.
